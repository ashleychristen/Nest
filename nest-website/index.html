Mission statement

To bridge the gap between hospital-based neonatal care and life at home by providing families with personalized education, practical caregiving support and continued access to healthcare and community resources

NEST is a proposed hospital-affiliated family support program, offered at no cost to eligible NICU families. Our goal is to ensure every family has access to the guidance, education and resources they need to feel confident bringing their baby home.

About Anne-Marie

With over 30 years of experience in pediatric and neonatal nursing, Anne-Marie brings extensive clinical knowledge and a passion for family-centred care to her role as NICU Family Navigator.

After spending much of her career caring for newborns and supporting families in the Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), Anne-Marie transitioned to a role focused on helping families feel prepared and supported as they bring their baby home.

Working alongside the hospital's neonatal healthcare team, Anne-Marie provides personalized home visits, caregiver education and connections to community resources throughout the transition home.

Your Journey with Anne-Marie

  1. Before discharge — Getting to know your family
    Anne-Marie meets parents in the NICU, learns about their concerns and reviews the discharge education and care plan with the clinical team.
  2. First 72 hours — Your first home check-in
    Anne-Marie contacts the family and arranges an initial home visit to discuss questions, feeding routines, medications and the adjustment to home care.
  3. Following weeks — Continued home support
    Regular visits provide opportunities to reinforce caregiving skills, discuss concerns, identify unmet needs and coordinate appropriate support.
  4. Ongoing — Connecting your family to resources and social supports.

As families become more confident, Anne-Marie helps transition them toward appropriate routine healthcare and community-based services.

HOW CAN A NAVIGATOR
HELP YOU AS A NEW
PARENT?

💡

Infant care Education

Helps parents understand and confidently perform everyday newborn care, including feeding, medication administration, safe sleep and hygiene.

🍼

Parental Guidance & Reassurance

Provides parents with an opportunity to ask questions, clarify medical instructions and better understand their baby's development.

⌂

Home & Family Assessment

Conducts home visits to identify practical caregiving challenges, assess family support needs and recognize potential barriers to a safe and manageable home environment.

♡

Care coordination & Connections

Maintains communication with baby's healthcare team, helps families navigate follow-ups and connects them with social workers, mental health services and community-based parenting programs.

Support that continues at home

From the first days after discharge through the following weeks, NEST helps families build confidence, ask questions and find the right supports for their changing needs.
